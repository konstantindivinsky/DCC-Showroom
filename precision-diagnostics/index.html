Frequently Asked Questions

Does a check engine light tell you what to replace?

No. Trouble codes identify affected systems or conditions. Testing is needed to determine the root cause before replacing components.

Can you diagnose intermittent electrical problems?

Intermittent faults may require testing under specific operating conditions or additional observation. A diagnostic evaluation helps establish the next step.

What should I do if a warning light is flashing?

A flashing check engine light can indicate an active misfire that may damage the catalytic converter. Reduce load, pull over when safe, and seek professional guidance. Red oil pressure or overheating warnings generally call for stopping safely and switching off the engine.
