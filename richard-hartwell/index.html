About

Experience, Perspective,
and a Client-First Approach

I'm Richard Hartwell, an independent attorney and advisor with more than 25 years of experience helping individuals, families, and business owners navigate important legal and strategic decisions.

My approach is straightforward: understand your goals, assess the risks, and provide clear, practical guidance tailored to your situation.

Every matter begins with a conversation. Whether the concern is a business decision, a family transition, or an unexpected dispute, the objective is to help you understand the options and move forward with confidence.

Clients can expect careful preparation, direct communication, and advice grounded in their broader priorities.
